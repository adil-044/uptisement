The problem

A new client finds you at 9pm, wants to book a cut, colour, or treatment, and your site asks them to call during open hours. A lot of them won’t wait.

The ones who do book can still forget. An empty chair is a quiet loss. And even happy clients often leave without a Google review, unless someone remembers to ask.

If booking, reminders and follow-up all depend on you, the day gets heavier than the work in the chair.

What we do for salons, spas and barbers

We build a booking-first website that makes it easy to book on a phone, then add simple automations if you want them. You see a free preview first, within 24 hours.

Websites start at $300. Hosting is $30 a month. Automations are priced on a 15-min call.

Which services fit

A website built around booking.

Your services, your location, and a clear booking button on the first screen, including after hours.

Booking reminders.

Clients get a reminder before their appointment, so you’re not chasing confirmations between appointments.

Review requests.

After a visit, a polite nudge to leave a Google review, without you writing the same text every time.

AI consulting.
